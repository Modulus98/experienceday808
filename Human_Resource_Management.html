What is Human Resource Management?

Every organization's most valuable asset isn't its machines or money — it's its people.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Human Resource Management (HRM) is the function that manages people — from hiring them, to developing them, to eventually letting them retire or move on.
🏢 Every company — big or small — needs someone looking after its people. That's HRM.
STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 2 / 6

Why HRM Matters to Every Business

🚀 The right people in the right roles drive productivity, innovation and growth.
📉 Poor people management leads to high staff turnover, low morale and wasted cost.
🌱 HRM isn't just paperwork — it shapes company culture, employee happiness, and long-term success.
STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 3 / 6

The 5 Core Functions of HRM

🎯Recruitment & Selection — finding & choosing the right people
📚Training & Development — growing employee skills
💰Compensation & Benefits — fair pay & rewards
📈Performance Management — reviewing & improving work
🤝Employee Relations — keeping the workplace fair & happy
🎮Today's game focuses on the first one!
STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 4 / 6

Zooming In: Recruitment & Selection

🔍 Recruitment is the process of attracting, screening and choosing the right candidate for the right job.
💸 Hiring the wrong person is costly — retraining, lost productivity, and a disrupted team.
🎯 Great recruiters match a candidate's background, skills and availability to what the job really needs.
