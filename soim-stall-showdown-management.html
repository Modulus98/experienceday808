🧠 These four jobs are called Planning, Organising, Leading and Controlling — every manager in every industry does them.
🏪 Today you will do all four while running a smoothie stall against a rival stall.
STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 2 / 6

Why All Four Matter

Drop any one of them and the day falls apart in a different way.

🗓️ No plan — you buy the wrong things and run out of money or stock.
👥 No organising — your best person ends up on the wrong job and the queue crawls.
💪 No leading — one argument or one sick friend stops everything.
🔍 No checking — you never find out which of those three actually cost you the money.

Why Checking Matters

Every hour you sell the smallest of three numbers.

🍓 How many cups of fruit you have left.
⚡ How many cups your crew can actually make.
🧍 How many customers chose your stall instead of your rival’s.
🔍 A manager finds out which one is holding them back, then fixes that one. Fixing the wrong thing costs money and changes nothing.

Careers & Technology in Management

💼 Careers: operations management, human resources, project management, retail management, production planning.
📡 Technology: staff rostering apps, point-of-sale systems, live dashboards and stock alerts all tell managers where the weak link is.
🎓 Studying Industrial Management prepares you to run the team, not just join it.

STEP 2 OF 3 · HOW TO PLAY

How to Play: Stall Showdown

$120 each, four friends, four hours. Every cup sells for $3.

1️⃣ Plan — split your money between fruit and your sign, then set your goal.
2️⃣ Organise — give each friend a job. The slowest job sets your speed.
3️⃣ Lead — three surprises hit both stalls. Pick your response before the clock ends.
4️⃣ Check — read your own numbers, find your weak link, fix it for the last hour.
5️⃣ ⏱️ Clock runs out = you get the worst option and −50 points.
🥤 Cup sold +10
🏆 Win an hour +200
⏱️ Per second left +3
🔍 Right diagnosis +150
