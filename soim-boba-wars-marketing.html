STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 2 / 6

Why You Cannot Sell to Everyone

Different people want different things from the same drink.

💸 A student wants the cheapest big cup. An office worker will pay double for real tea, but only near work.
🪧 One poster cannot say “cheapest” and “most premium” at the same time and be believed.
✂️ So you choose a target market — and choosing means deciding who you are not for.

STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 4 / 6

The Promise and the Cup

This is the big one, and it is where real brands go wrong.

🚩 Your poster is a promise. It decides who walks in the first time.
🧋 Your cup is what you actually serve. It decides whether anybody comes back.
🤝 Promise more than you serve and you get one busy week, then an empty shop. Businesses call the gap a loss of trust.
STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 5 / 6

Careers & Technology in Marketing

💼 Careers: brand management, social media and content, market research, retail buying, product management.
📡 Technology: ad managers, analytics dashboards, loyalty apps and review data show exactly which customers came back and which did not.
🎓 Every business needs someone who understands the customer better than the competition does.

STEP 2 OF 3 · HOW TO PLAY

How to Play: Boba Wars

$500 each and four weeks. Rent comes out every week.

1️⃣ Who — pick the group your shop is for (one, or two if you dare).
2️⃣ What and how much — set your price, your poster and what goes in the cup.
3️⃣ Where and how — choose your street, then spend 6 tokens on telling people.
4️⃣ Four weeks — three surprises hit the street. Answer before the clock ends.
5️⃣ ⏱️ Clock runs out = worst option and −50 points.
🧋 Cup sold +3
🏆 Win a week +150
💵 Per $ profit +2
🤝 Trust 70%+ +200
