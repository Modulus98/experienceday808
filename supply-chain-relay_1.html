📦 Supply Chain Management (SCM) is how businesses plan, move and control goods from raw material to customer.
🌍 It touches almost every industry — food, fashion, electronics, medicine and more.
STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 2 / 6

Why the Order of Stages Matters

Each item must move through the chain in the right sequence.

🔀 Skip a stage or send goods to the wrong place, and the whole chain slows down.
⏳ Raw material can't reach a customer directly — it must be made, stored, then sold first.
✅ Getting the sequence right means products arrive faster and cheaper for everyone.

Why Sorting Correctly Matters

🏭➡️🏪 Real logistics teams route thousands of items every day — one wrong route causes delays.
💸 A misrouted shipment means extra transport cost, wasted time, and unhappy customers.
🎯 That's why, in this game, a wrong or missed item costs you points — just like it costs a real business!
STEP 1 OF 3 · LEARN SLIDE 5 / 6

Careers & Technology in SCM

💼 Careers: warehouse operations, logistics planning, procurement, retail buying, delivery management.
📡 Technology: barcodes, RFID tags, warehouse robots and tracking apps all help move goods faster and more accurately.
🎓 Studying Business / Supply Chain Management opens doors into almost any industry.
