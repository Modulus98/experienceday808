What is International Business?

A company selling, producing or partnering across borders — not just in one country.

🏪 Your favourite shop probably started as one small store in a single country. Now its products are bought all over the world.
🎒 Analogy: a local business is like a student who only ever studies in their hometown. An international business is that same student getting a passport, learning new languages and making friends in other countries — reaching far more opportunities than staying home ever could.

2 Big Reasons Companies Go International

📈 Market expansion. When the home market gets full or is simply too small, companies look for more customers in other countries to keep growing. More countries means more potential buyers.
🎭 Cultural adaptation. Smart companies change their products, colours, flavours and messages to fit local culture — like McDonald’s selling rice sets in Asia — so people in that country actually accept them.

🍫 Fun fact: most of the cocoa beans in a KitKat come from farms in Côte d’Ivoire in West Africa, but get turned into chocolate in factories in Switzerland or Malaysia before reaching your hands. That is a global supply chain at work.
🍔 Fun fact: McDonald’s in India sells zero beef products — the menu is built around chicken, paneer and vegetables. A textbook example of cultural adaptation.

Careers & Technology in International Business

💼 Careers: export and import management, global sourcing, freight forwarding, trade compliance, international marketing, regional brand management.
📡 Technology: shipment tracking platforms, customs and tariff systems, currency exchange tools and translation software let a small team run sales across dozens of countries at once.
🎓 Malaysia sits on one of the world’s busiest trade routes, so this knowledge is in demand right where you live.
